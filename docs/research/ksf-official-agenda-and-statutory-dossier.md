## 4. The Four Operational Wings (Institutional Agenda)

The Kiran Singh Foundation operates **4 dedicated operational wings** focused on bringing measurable, structural change to society (PDF Pages 2–3):

### Wing A: KSF (Student Welfare) Wing
- **Cohort Strength & Merit Selection:** Exactly **40 students** selected by the foundation through a rigorous **psychometric test and formal interview** organized by the core members and conducted by accomplished professionals excelling in their respective fields.
- **Dual Internship Tracks:** Offers two structured residencies—the **Summer Internship Program** and the **Winter Internship Program**—designed to forge a solid personality by helping students discover their true potential and integrate soft skills into their character.
- **4-Team Supervisory Architecture:** The 40 scholars are organized into **4 teams**, with each team led by a three-tier supervisory hierarchy:
  1. **Head Superintendent Manager** (Tier 1)
  2. **Dy. Head Superintendent Manager** (Tier 2)
  3. **Team Leader** (Tier 3)
- **Research Methodology, Plagiarism Audit & Panel Defense:**
  - Each team is assigned diverse research topics and prepares a formal **Research Report** alongside a **PowerPoint Presentation**.
  - Teams defend their findings before an evaluative panel of top professionals.
  - Every research report must adhere to strict academic formatting and undergoes a mandatory **plagiarism report audit** to measure copying percentage, verifying credibility and original authorship.
- **Expert Technical & Confidence Sessions:** Regular masterclasses conducted by expert members of the foundation to instill executive confidence and deepen technical knowledge.
- **Internship Closing Ceremony & Stipend Honors:** Each internship cycle concludes with a formal **Internship Closing Ceremony**, where scholars are honored with **Certificates, Trophies, Mementos, and a Stipend amount** recognizing their dedication and hard work.

### Wing B: KSF (Dog Welfare) Wing
- **Rescue, Medical Triage & Vaccination:** Street dogs are rescued, medically treated, and systematically vaccinated by the foundation to protect them from diseases and road accidents, ensuring a dignified standard of living.
- **4 Dedicated Shelter Homes:** The foundation has constructed **4 shelter homes** in Prayagraj currently housing **34 rescued street dogs** in total.
- **Complete Provisioning:** Daily nutrition (food), clean water facilities, safe shelter, and veterinary medical care are 100% funded and managed by the foundation.

### Wing C: KSF (Village Development) Wing
- **Core Mandate:** *“Empowering village through education.”*
- **Rural Educational Equity:** Students residing in rural villages are provided comprehensive educational resources and communication training so they can express themselves effectively and compete with real-life challenges.
- **Full Financial & Residential Coverage:** The Kiran Singh Foundation underwrites **100% of their educational fees, daily food costs, and shelter facilities**.

### Wing D: KSF (Women Empowerment) Wing
- **Career & Entrepreneurial Acceleration:** Female students and skilled women are provided structured opportunities to build resilient careers across **education, business, and entrepreneurship**.
- **Awareness Programs & Investment Summits:** The foundation organizes targeted awareness campaigns and **investment summits** designed to mentor, fund, and motivate young women to pursue independent professional careers despite familial or societal pressures.
