## 2. Institutional Heritage & Historical Continuity

1. **Foundational Inception:**
   - Established in sacred memory of **Late Kiran Singh ji**, a distinguished doctoral ethologist and behavioral scientist who conducted pioneering research on apex felids (lions, tigers, leopards) and served as an Analyst with the Tamil Nadu Zoo Authority at Arignar Anna Zoological Park (AAZP Vandalur, Chennai) during the tenure of Chief Minister Late J. Jayalalithaa ("Amma").
2. **Statutory Trust Establishment:**
   - Formally constituted and established on **30th of April 2023** as an irrevocable charitable trust under the **Indian Trust Act of 1882**.
3. **Four-Wing Operational Mandate:**
   - **KSF (Student Welfare) Wing:** Direct sponsorship and soft-skills incubation for 40 scholars (4 teams).
   - **KSF (Dog Welfare) Wing:** Lifelong shelter, nourishment, sterilization, and vaccination for 34 rescued street dogs at the Prayagraj facility.
   - **KSF (Village Development) Wing:** Rural education endowments, digital learning labs, and fee sponsorships.
   - **KSF (Women Empowerment) Wing:** Financial self-reliance seminars, vocational workshops, and healthcare access.
